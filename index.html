    Conditions

    Online, both drivers race in the same time of day and weather. A stranger is matched with someone who picked the same.

    Your car

    With tyre wear on, stop inside the blue box on the right of the main straight (about 100 m after the line) for 3 seconds to fit a fresh set.

    Crash into walls or rival cars and your car gets damaged. Heavy crashes bring out the yellow flag, the safety car and a recovery truck. Drive into the blue PIT box to repair.

    Online races use Race handling and have no crash damage, because the two cars cannot touch.
